# Q1 Marketing Campaigns Customer Feedback Summary

## Campaign: "Future Ready AI" (January - February)
- **Positive Feedback**: 78% of customers praised the new webinar series. Many highlighted that the practical AI deployment guides were highly actionable.
- **Constructive Criticism**: Some enterprise clients mentioned that the pricing tiers were confusing and required too many meetings to clarify.
- **Overall Sentiment**: Highly positive, driving a 15% increase in inbound sales leads compared to Q4.

## Campaign: "Presidio Secure" (March)
- **Positive Feedback**: The whitepaper on AI Data Handling was downloaded 12,000 times. Customers appreciated the deep dive into SOC2 compliance.
- **Constructive Criticism**: Several mid-market customers felt the enterprise focus alienated smaller startups. They requested more case studies for businesses under 50 employees.
- **Overall Sentiment**: Moderately positive, though conversion rates were slightly lower than expected due to market misalignment.

*Note: All PII and PHI has been scrubbed from this report in accordance with Presidio AI Data Handling policies.*
