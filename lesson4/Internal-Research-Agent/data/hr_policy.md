# Presidio Global HR Policy

## Leave Policy
Employees are entitled to 20 days of paid time off (PTO) annually. Additionally, employees receive 10 days of sick leave. Unused sick leave does not roll over to the next calendar year.

## Remote Work Policy
Presidio offers a flexible hybrid work environment. Employees may work remotely up to 3 days a week. Core hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in the employee's local time zone, during which employees must be available for meetings.

## Performance Reviews
Performance reviews occur bi-annually in Q2 and Q4. Feedback from the Q1 and Q3 check-ins is incorporated into the formal review.
