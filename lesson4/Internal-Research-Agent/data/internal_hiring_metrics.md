# Presidio Internal Hiring Metrics & Trends (2024-2025)

## Recruitment Velocity
- **Time-to-Hire**: Currently averaging **48 days** across all departments. This is a slight increase from our 42-day average last year.
- **Interviews per Hire**: On average, candidates go through **4 interview rounds** before an offer is extended.
- **Offer Acceptance Rate**: We currently maintain a highly competitive **88% offer acceptance rate**.

## Team Capacity & Focus
- **Recruiting Team Size**: We currently have 15 full-time recruiters.
- **Strategic Focus**: The primary hiring focus for Q1/Q2 is expanding the Machine Learning Engineering team and the Enterprise Sales division. We are prioritizing candidate quality and cultural fit over hiring speed.

## Retention
- **First-Year Retention Rate**: 91% of new hires remain with Presidio beyond their first 12 months.
