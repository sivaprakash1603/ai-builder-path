# Presidio Data Compliance & AI Data Handling Policy

## AI Data Handling
When using Artificial Intelligence tools, employees must ensure no Personally Identifiable Information (PII) or Protected Health Information (PHI) is submitted to public or unauthorized AI models.
All customer feedback related to marketing campaigns must be anonymized before analysis via AI.

## Security Standards
Presidio complies with SOC2 Type II standards. Data at rest must be encrypted using AES-256. Access to internal production databases requires Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) and is strictly logged.
